The Mag

Icon, paper, light.

This project presents a magazine entirely dedicated to The Weeknd, conceived from scratch, along with its presentation video. For the design, I stayed true to the artist's visual universe with an art direction centred on black, white and red — a direct nod to his iconic jacket. The final result comprises the full editorial work and a promotional video, blending 3D and 2D animations to showcase the pages and the magazine concept in a modern and dynamic way.
