TeamSpeak

Signal, colour, return.

The goal of this project was to give TeamSpeak's visual identity a fresh look. The result takes the form of a poster announcing the fictional return of the platform with a modernised art direction. Visually, I preserved the brand's DNA while energising its codes: the classic blue becomes more electric, contrasted against the characteristic green of the voice activity indicator. To engage the community, the poster also includes easter eggs — subtle, hidden references that only seasoned users of the platform will be able to spot.
